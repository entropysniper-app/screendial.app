Display controls

How do I get my old resolution back?

When you try a new resolution, choose Revert or wait ten seconds. Choose Keep if it looks right. A resolution you’ve kept before applies immediately. Use Undo in the change notice to switch back.

Why is a size or refresh rate missing?

ScreenDial offers the modes macOS reports for the connected display. If only one size is available, the slider has no other step. Open the panel’s More (…) menu and choose System Display Settings… to check the options for that screen.

Why does my monitor’s brightness setting stay the same?

ScreenDial dims the image in software. Use your monitor’s own controls to change its backlight. To remove software dimming, choose More (…) → Reset Brightness. ScreenDial keeps at least one display visible so you can reach the controls.

Why does the screen look warmer than expected?

ScreenDial’s warmth and macOS Night Shift can both tint the picture. Turn off Warmth in the panel to see the difference. Choose the Manual schedule if you want to control warmth yourself. With Dark Appearance follows your Mac’s appearance changes.

Scenes and shortcuts

Where did my saved scene go?

Scenes belong to a particular combination of displays. Reconnect the screens you used when you saved the scene. In Settings → Scenes, you can see named scenes grouped by display setup. To keep a different setup, connect those displays and choose Save as Scene in the panel.

Why does a brightness shortcut affect the other screen?

Move the pointer onto the display you want to adjust before using Brighter or Dimmer. If a shortcut does nothing, check that ScreenDial is running, then open Settings → Shortcuts and choose a key combination that’s free.
