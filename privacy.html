The Mac app

ScreenDial works offline without an account. The app does not collect or transmit personal data and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

What stays on your Mac

ScreenDial uses display names and identifiers supplied by macOS to recognize a setup when you reconnect it. Its local library stores scene names, resolution and refresh rate, brightness, arrangement, mirroring, and warmth settings. The app also saves preferences such as shortcut assignments in its local settings.

You can review and delete individual named scenes in Settings → Scenes. For a disconnected setup listed there, Forget These Displays removes that setup’s saved scenes and display settings.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and their images. GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page and may log it for security. This site uses no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party embeds.

GitHub explains how it handles visitor information in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Public support issues

If you open an issue or comment, your GitHub username, message, and any attachments are publicly visible. ScreenDial does not automatically send settings or diagnostics to support. Share only the details you want to make public.

For questions about this policy, email entropysniper@gmail.com or visit the ScreenDial public issue tracker.
